# Fonts

The pictures and the GRUB fonts are made from DejaVu Sans Mono
(https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/). DejaVu fonts are free to use, change and
pass on under the Bitstream Vera and Arev licences, see
https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html. The font files themselves are not
included; only glyph masks and GRUB bitmap fonts made from them.
