The NIRUX look for the start of the computer.

- The GRUB menu with the NIRUX banner, "A MINIMAL X11 DESKTOP" and the cursor,
  and every installed kernel straight in the menu.
- The boot splash (Plymouth): the banner typed in as on the lock screen, and
  the disk password asked at `$ unlock disk`, with `[ .. ] unlocking` and
  `[ fail ] wrong password, try again`.
- Made on the machine for its screen and the NIRUX theme's colours; safe to
  run again; `nirusplash remove` puts the plain boot back. Debian, Void and
  Arch with GRUB.
